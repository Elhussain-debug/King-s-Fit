# KINGSFIT Website Design Brief

## Project Goal

Design a premium conversion-focused website for **Kingsfit**, a Nigerian menswear brand.

The website must focus on only four key messages:

1. What the brand is  
2. Who it is for  
3. Why it is different  
4. What to buy

The final result should feel premium, masculine, luxurious, modern, and built for high-value customers.

---

# Brand Overview

**Kingsfit** is a Nigerian menswear brand specializing in:

- Native wear  
- Formal wear  
- Executive menswear  
- Custom tailoring

Kingsfit is for men who understand that appearance matters.

---

# Target Audience

Primary audience:

- Executives  
- Business owners  
- Professionals  
- Grooms  
- Stylish men aged 25–50

Psychology:

- Values image
- Wants respect
- Pays for quality
- Prefers premium service
- Wants confidence through appearance

---

# Brand Personality

Use these feelings throughout the design:

- Powerful  
- Elegant  
- Masculine  
- Elite  
- Refined  
- Quiet confidence  
- Premium

Think:

**Tom Ford meets Lagos executive culture**

---

# Design Direction

## Style

Luxury editorial website.

## Colors

- Background: Deep black / charcoal  
- Secondary background: Dark navy  
- Text: White / soft white  
- Accent: Gold  
- Optional secondary accent: Deep green

## Typography

### Heading Font

Elegant serif font.

Examples:

- Cormorant Garamond  
- Libre Baskerville

### Body Font

Modern clean sans-serif.

Examples:

- DM Sans  
- Inter  
- Jost

# Layout Principles

- Large spacing  
- Strong typography  
- Minimal clutter  
- Premium imagery  
- High contrast  
- Smooth scrolling  
- Mobile-first responsive design

---

# Interactions

- Smooth fade-in animations  
- Hover zoom on product cards  
- Gold hover accents  
- Sticky navbar  
- Smooth section scrolling

---

# Mobile Experience

Must feel equally premium.

Prioritize:

- Large text  
- Fast load speed  
- Clean navigation  
- Easy WhatsApp ordering  
- Thumb-friendly buttons

---

# Copywriting Tone

Use short confident copy.

Avoid hype language.

Speak with authority.

Examples:

Bad:
Best fashion designer in Nigeria!!!

Good:
Tailored for men who understand standards.

---

# Conversion Goal

Primary conversions:

1. WhatsApp inquiry  
2. Book fitting  
3. Browse collection

---

# Important Rule

This should NOT feel like a tailor website.

It should feel like a luxury menswear brand.
